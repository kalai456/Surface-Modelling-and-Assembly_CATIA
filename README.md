# TITLE OF THE PRODUCT
 WATER CAP
 

# DESCRIPTION
 While creating the water cap model in the Surface Design workbench of CATIA, the focus was mainly on shaping smooth and curved surfaces. I started by defining basic profiles and then used surface tools to build the outer shape of the cap. Blending and joining operations were important to get a clean, continuous surface without gaps. It took some effort to maintain proper curvature and smooth transitions. After completing the surfaces, they were refined to achieve a realistic cap shape. Overall, it helped me understand how complex shapes are created using surface modeling techniques.
 

# CHALLENGES FACED TO DEVELOP
 Creating smooth curved surfaces was not easy. Maintaining proper continuity between surfaces required careful adjustments. Sometimes surfaces did not join properly, leading to small gaps. Trimming and blending operations needed precision to get the correct shape. Controlling the curvature of the top grip portion was also challenging. There were alignment issues while positioning profiles. Visualizing the final shape from multiple surfaces took some practice. Overall, achieving a clean and smooth finish was the main difficulty.
